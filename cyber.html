About

OT security, from the network up

I'm a Franco-Finnish OT/ICS security architect. My OT work spans a nuclear environment at AREVA NP, industrial and critical-infrastructure clients at Tietoevry, IEC 62443-4-1/4-2 product security on the supplier side, and shipboard systems — from bridge watches at sea to a classification society inspection at a shipyard.

Before OT, I spent two decades building carrier-grade networks for Nokia Siemens Networks, Telenor and DE-CIX. That is not an IT detour: most OT compromises arrive through the IT boundary, a remote-access path or a supplier connection, and I design that boundary knowing exactly how both sides of it are built.

Based in Kaarina, Finland, with full EU work rights.
