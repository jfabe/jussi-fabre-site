Independent research

Sovereignty on the Seabed

Subsea infrastructure, hybrid threats and crisis resilience in the Baltic Sea — a policy briefing forthcoming in Centrum Balticum’s BSR Policy Briefing series. Written on my own initiative and independently of any employer or client, it brings together the three worlds this site is about: networks, ships and critical infrastructure.

Between October 2023 and January 2026, subsea cables and a gas pipeline in the Baltic Sea were damaged in at least eight incidents, most of them by a merchant ship dragging its anchor. The briefing reads these incidents from three angles at once and asks what Finland would need if many international links were lost at the same time. It is strictly evidence-based and does not attribute the incidents to any state.

01 — The network

How the Internet routed around the cuts

Analysis of public RIPE Atlas and RIS measurements: traffic rerouted largely without packet loss, but a second winter of cuts produced the first signs of congestion — resilience that depends on many alternatives staying available.
