About

Two regulated worlds, one architecture

I'm a Franco-Finnish cybersecurity architect with 25+ years spanning OT/ICS security, network architecture, and multi-cloud infrastructure across telecom, industrial, and maritime environments.

That maritime part isn't a hobby on the side — it's an active STCW A-II/1 deck officer qualification with hands-on sea service in the Baltic and North Sea. Ships are safety-critical, tightly regulated, cyber-physical systems, which is exactly the world that IEC 62443, NIS2, and the incoming IACS UR E26/E27 cyber requirements are asking industrial and maritime operators to take seriously. I bring both halves: the architecture and governance, and the operational instinct for how these systems behave under real conditions.

Based in Kaarina, Finland, with full EU work rights. Currently advancing CISSP and IEC 62443 certifications.
