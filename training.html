Track 1 · 6 weeks · from 26 Oct 2026

Maritime OT security programme

The aim is hands-on fluency in ship OT: the class rules that govern it, the protocols that run on board, and the segmentation that protects them. It builds on existing OT foundations (nuclear OT/IT support at AREVA NP, industrial and utility clients at CGI, OT/IT architecture at Tietoevry, sea service and a RINA class survey) and adds a documented lab.

In progress 8–10 h / week Lab on GitHub

IACS UR E26 — the ship

WEEK 1

Full read of E26: the five functions (identify, protect, detect, respond, recover), required documents per design, construction and operation phase, and who owns each one — yard, integrator, owner or class.

→ E26 requirements matrix IACS UR E26 · ships contracted from 1 Jul 2024

E27, DNV rules & BIMCO

WEEK 2

Supplier obligations under E27 and their link to IEC 62443-4-2; DNV class rules and notations (Cyber secure, Essential, Advanced); the owner's view in BIMCO; the IMO framework.

→ Memo: where DNV goes beyond the IACS minimum UR E27 · DNV-RU-SHIP Pt.6 Ch.5 · DNV-CG-0325 · DNV-RP-0496 · BIMCO v5 · MSC-FAL.1/Circ.3/Rev.3

Navigation & NMEA lab

WEEK 3

OpenCPN with simulated NMEA data, traffic captured in Wireshark (GGA, RMC, HDT, AIS), and a spoofed GPS sentence accepted without authentication. Principles of IEC 61162-450 and the 61162-460 secure gateway.

→ NMEA lab and spoofing test NMEA 0183 · IEC 61162-450 / -460

Automation & segmentation lab

WEEK 4

A simulated ballast pump on OpenPLC over Modbus TCP, unauthenticated register writes, then an IT zone and an OT zone with a firewalled conduit and passive monitoring (Zeek / Suricata) on the OT side.

→ Documented lab on GitHub, mapped to E26 Modbus TCP · IEC 62443-3-2 zones & conduits

Fathom assessment demo

WEEK 5

Fathom's questions checked against the E26 matrix and gaps filled, then a full assessment of a sample ro-pax (IAS, PMS, ECDIS, cargo system), linked to Conduit and the lab segmentation.

→ Five-minute E26 assessment demo IACS UR E26 · IEC 62443

Case studies & portfolio

WEEK 6

Five written case studies (challenge, action, result) on nuclear OT, a class survey at a shipyard, industrial clients, IT/OT boundary design and life on the bridge, plus an OT-first profile.

→ OT case studies and updated profile Portfolio
