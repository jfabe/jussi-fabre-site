Why this mix

Three disciplines in one building

Most data centre teams have specialists for each layer: network engineers for interconnection, MEP and BMS people for the building, compliance for the regulator. The difficult part sits between them, where a cooling system's remote access meets the security model, or a fibre route meets the resilience requirements.

That in-between work is where this profile fits: someone who has operated interconnection at one of the world's largest internet exchanges, designed security for industrial and critical-infrastructure sites, and can read the regulation and the contract with the same eye.

Site networks & OT security

The plant behind the racks

Tenants buy uptime, but uptime depends on power, cooling and safety systems that are industrial OT — often installed by a dozen integrators, each with its own remote access. Designing those networks as zones and conduits from day one is cheaper than retrofitting them after the first audit.
