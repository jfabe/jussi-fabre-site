About

Deck officer, built for regulated environments

I hold an STCW A-II/1 Officer of the Watch certificate of competency and have served on RoRo and cargo vessels in the Baltic and North Sea, including Viking Cinderella, Finnstar, Finnfellow, Finnsky, Finnmerchant, Finntide, Finnbreeze, and Hjördis.

Alongside watchkeeping and cargo operations, I served as watch officer during a periodic RINA classification survey at a shipyard in southern Sweden — direct exposure to hull, machinery, and fire-safety inspection and dry-docking processes. That, combined with a B.Eng. in Maritime Studies from Novia University of Applied Sciences, is the foundation for newbuilding, quality, and classification-adjacent work.

Based in Kaarina, Finland. Full STCW safety certificate package maintained throughout — Basic Safety Training, Advanced Fire Fighting, Medical Care, Survival Craft & Rescue Boats, and Designated Security Duties.
