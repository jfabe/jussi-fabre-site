About

Advisory · Ship networks & OT security

From class requirement to a network that sails

Since IACS UR E26 and E27 became mandatory for ships contracted from 1 July 2024, cyber resilience is a class condition, not an IT wish-list. Owners, yards and system integrators now have to prove that propulsion, DP, bridge and alarm networks are segmented, documented and maintainable — while the vessel keeps to its schedule.

The advisory service bridges three worlds that rarely share a vocabulary: the bridge team that depends on the systems, the class surveyor who approves them, and the suppliers who build them. The result is a vendor-neutral architecture that passes survey first time and that the crew can actually live with.

01 · Assess

Where does the vessel or fleet stand today?

  • Gap analysis against UR E26 / E27 and IEC 62443
  • OT asset inventory and zone & conduit model
  • Vessel-to-shore and vendor remote-access risk review
  • Prioritised findings, sized for budget and dry-dock windows

Outcome: a clear, costed picture for management and class.

02 · Design

What should the network look like?

  • Reference architecture — segmentation, redundant rings, QoS, firewalled conduits
  • Vendor-neutral selection of type-approved switches, routers and IEC 62443-4-2 components
  • Connectivity strategy — 4G / 5G / 450 MHz, satellite, WLAN
  • Secure remote-access model for service partners

Outcome: a specification yards and suppliers can quote against.

03 · Deliver & verify

How does it reach approval — and stay compliant?

  • Owner's representative between yard, suppliers and class
  • Supplier E27 documentation review, FAT / SAT witnessing
  • Class survey preparation and finding closure
  • Crew handover, change management and lifecycle patching plan

Outcome: approval on schedule and a vessel that stays compliant.

The added value

  • Fewer surprises at survey — requirements addressed in design, not discovered in sea trials
  • Independent advice — no hardware to sell, so recommendations follow the vessel's needs
  • Operational realism — controls designed by someone who has stood the watch they protect
  • One interlocutor — Finnish, French and English across owners, yards and class
  • Retrofit-aware — reuse of existing cabling and hardware where it is safe to do so
